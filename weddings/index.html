Questions

We already have a video invite. Do we need this?

They work together. Add the website link at the end of your video invite so guests get maps, timings and RSVP in one tap.

Will older relatives manage?

Yes. It opens like any link on WhatsApp, with no app or login. "Directions" opens Google Maps and RSVP opens WhatsApp.

Can we keep guest details private?

Yes. We never show guest names publicly, and we can leave sensitive details (like room numbers) out of the public page.

Can we use our own domain like aanyawedsvihaan.com?

Yes. You buy the domain in your own name (usually under ₹1,000 a year) and we connect it for free.
