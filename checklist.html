Parent Safety Checklist

For NRIs with elderly parents in India. Go through it together on your next video call, which takes about 20 minutes.

📱 Phone

  • Screen lock is on (PIN or fingerprint), and they know it but nobody else does
  • Phone and apps are updated
  • Spam call filter is on (in the phone's call settings)
  • Apps are installed only from the Play Store / App Store, and "install unknown apps" is switched off
  • No screen-sharing or "remote support" apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer and similar) unless you put them there
  • Emergency contacts are saved on the lock screen / emergency SOS is set up

💸 UPI & Banking

  • A daily UPI limit is set to a sensible amount in their UPI app
  • They know: you never need a PIN to RECEIVE money
  • SMS and email alerts for every transaction are on, and you get a copy too if possible
  • They know the bank will never ask for an OTP, PIN or CVV on a call
  • The bank's real customer-care number is saved under the name "BANK – REAL"
  • They know how to block their card or UPI quickly, and the number is written down

💬 WhatsApp

  • Two-step verification is on (Settings → Account)
  • Calls from unknown numbers are silenced (Settings → Privacy → Calls)
  • Only contacts can add them to groups
  • Profile photo is visible to contacts only

⚠️ The 6 scams to explain to them

  • "Your KYC has expired" SMS or call with a link → never click it, call the bank yourself
  • "Digital arrest": a video call from fake police/CBI/customs → real police never arrest anyone on video calls. Hang up.
  • "Courier has illegal items in your name" → hang up
  • "Your son/daughter is in trouble, send money now" → hang up and call your child directly
  • "Electricity will be cut tonight, call this number" → check with the official electricity board
  • "You've won a prize / refund, share the OTP" → never share an OTP

The one rule for them: "If someone on the phone makes me scared or rushes me, I hang up and call my child."

If money is lost, call the national cyber-crime helpline 1930 immediately and report at cybercrime.gov.in. Speed matters.

🏠 Home & health

  • Medicines list with doses is written down and kept on the fridge
  • Doctor, pharmacy and hospital numbers are on the same sheet
  • A trusted neighbour has your number and theirs
  • Health insurance policy number and TPA helpline are written down
  • Pension life certificate due date is known
